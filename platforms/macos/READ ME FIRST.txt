Botanical Network 0.1.0 for Mac  -  Delhi Metro map with your saved places
==========================================================================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER

  Botanical Network_0.1.0_universal.dmg   the app, for Apple Silicon and Intel
                                          Macs.
  places/delhi-all-places.json            every place from the sender's own map,
                                          with exit gates and categories. Drop
                                          this one file onto the map first.
  places/                                 the smaller lists it was built from,
                                          plus a tiny sample file.

INSTALL

  1. Double-click the .dmg and drag Botanical Network into Applications.
  2. The app is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch
     ("Apple could not verify..."). Click Done, then open System Settings >
     Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to
     Botanical Network. This only happens once.
     On macOS 14 or earlier you can instead Control-click the app in
     Applications and choose Open.

FIRST RUN

  - Drop places/delhi-all-places.json onto the map, or press "Import places..."
    and pick it. A checklist shows what came in. (Importing the smaller lists
    afterwards just reports those places as "already exists".)
  - Hover a station to see the places saved there; click "Start here" on one
    station and "End here" on another to plan a journey.
  - Press the "i" button next to Import to copy a prompt you can paste into
    any AI chat with your own list of places; save its reply as a .json file
    and import it.
  - Your places are stored in
    ~/Library/Application Support/com.botanicalnetwork.desktop.
    "Export..." writes them to a file you can send to someone else.

Runs on macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.
